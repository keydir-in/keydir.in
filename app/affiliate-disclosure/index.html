SECTION_01 What This Is

This disclosure explains how the KeyDir Application may participate in affiliate programs. Where the platform includes affiliate links, those links may earn the platform a commission if you buy through them. This disclosure is made to keep that relationship transparent.

SECTION_02 When Links May Be Affiliate

Not every link on the platform is affiliate. Links may be affiliate where they lead to a product or service sold by a third party, such as a keyboard, set of keycaps, or vendor storefront that participates in an affiliate program. Links to the vendor's own official website are never affiliate in nature.

SECTION_03 How Affiliate Links Are Labeled

Affiliate links are always identified, either with a visible label such as "affiliate link" or "may earn commission" next to the link, or through a clear disclosure banner on the page containing them. No affiliate link is hidden.

SECTION_04 What Never Changes

✓Affiliate links never affect which products are listed or recommended.
✓Affiliate participation never affects ranking, votes, or review content.
✓Sponsorship and advertising never influence organic listings — see the Advertising Policy.
✓We do not take payment in exchange for positive reviews or ratings.

SECTION_05 No Extra Cost to You

If you buy through an affiliate link, you do not pay more. The commission is paid by the seller out of their normal margin. Your purchase price is unaffected.

SECTION_06 Contact

Questions about this disclosure can be sent via the Contact page or to officialkeydir.in@gmail.com.
