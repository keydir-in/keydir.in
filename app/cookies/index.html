The KeyDir Platform does not use cookies for advertising or cross-site tracking. The only cookie-like storage is a theme preference and app settings saved in your browser's local storage.

SECTION_01 What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a website. They are widely used to remember preferences, keep you signed in, or track activity. "Local storage" is a similar browser mechanism that stores data on your device.

SECTION_02 Cookies We Use

We do not set first-party tracking cookies on the application. The table below reflects current behavior:

Cookie / StoragePurposeTypeDuration
keydir-themeRemembers your dark/light theme preferenceLocal storageUntil cleared
Analytics cookiesRecognize returning visitors for aggregate statisticsThird-partySet by provider

No cookie is used for advertising, cross-site tracking, or selling data.

SECTION_03 Local Storage

The application stores values in your browser's local storage, such as your theme preference (dark or light) and app settings. These are only used to render the platform with your chosen appearance on return visits. They never leave your device.

SECTION_04 Analytics Cookies

The platform runs a lightweight, privacy-respecting analytics script that reports aggregate page views and traffic sources. The provider may use cookies to identify returning visitors. This data is aggregated and cannot be used to personally identify you.

SECTION_05 Managing Cookies

You can control or delete cookies and storage at any time in your browser settings:

  • Delete — clear all site data from your browser's site-settings panel.
  • Block — block third-party cookies or disable JavaScript to stop analytics.
  • Private mode — browse in a private/incognito window and data will not persist.
  • Extensions — use a tracker blocker to stop analytics requests entirely.

SECTION_06 Third-Party Cookies

Links from the platform lead to external websites (vendors, builders, brands, GitHub, and the analytics provider). Those websites operate under their own cookie policies, which we do not control. Review their policies before using them.

SECTION_07 Changes to This Policy

If we ever introduce a cookie or storage mechanism, this policy will be updated first. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
