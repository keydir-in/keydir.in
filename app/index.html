THE PLATFORM

3 PILLARS
PILLAR_001

Official Application

app.keydir.in is the official application for the KeyDir platform, operated by KeyDir. It provides accounts, collections, listings, pricing tools and community features on top of the directory.

PILLAR_002

Transparent Monetization

Some links may be affiliate links and sponsored listings are always identified. Advertising and paid placement never guarantee or influence ranking, votes, or search results.

PILLAR_003

Verify Before You Buy

Prices, availability and product specifications are community and vendor sourced and may not always be real-time. Always confirm details on the vendor's own storefront before purchasing.
