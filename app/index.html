HOW KEYDIR WORKS

3 STEPS
STEP_001

Community-Maintained Data

Vendors, builders and brands are curated by the community and maintainers on GitHub. Anyone can submit a correction, suggestion or new listing through issues and pull requests.

STEP_002

Verified, Neutral Directory

Entries are reviewed for accuracy and legitimacy. KEYDIR has no affiliate links, no sponsored placements and no ads — nothing is ranked or hidden for money.

STEP_003

Free For Everyone

The directory and its app are free to use. Transactions happen directly between you and the vendor or builder — KEYDIR is a directory, not a marketplace, broker or escrow.
