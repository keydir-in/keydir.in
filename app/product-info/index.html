SECTION_01 Where Product Information Comes From

Product specifications, images, and descriptions shown on the platform are compiled from multiple sources. They are never a substitute for the manufacturer's or vendor's official product information.

SECTION_02 Community and Vendor Sourced

  • Vendor information — data provided or confirmed by the vendor selling the product.
  • Community contributions — specifications submitted by community members and reviewed before display.
  • Manufacturer pages — official product pages used for cross-checking.

Where a source is known, it may be noted on the product page. Because information is compiled from these sources, it can be incomplete, out of date, or wrong.

SECTION_03 Verify Before You Buy

⚠
Verify product specifications on the manufacturer's or vendor's official page before purchasing. The platform cannot guarantee that any specification, image, or description is accurate, current, or complete — particularly for products you intend to spend money on.

SECTION_04 The Platform's Limits

Product information is provided for general reference and comparison. It does not form part of any offer, warranty, or guarantee. The KeyDir Application does not sell products and is not a party to any purchase. Full limits are in the Disclaimer.

SECTION_05 Reporting Errors

Found a wrong specification? Report it via the Report Abuse page (reason: "product information") or the Contact page. Include the product page and the correct information with a source link.
