SECTION_02 What to Send Where

Listing correction
Submit through the app or email with the listing URL and the correction, backed by a link to the vendor's current page.
Report a vendor
Use the Report Abuse page — describe the experience, dates, and amounts involved.
Privacy / data removal
Use the Data Removal page or email officialkeydir.in@gmail.com.
Copyright
Follow the notice requirements on the DMCA page and email officialkeydir.in@gmail.com.
Security
Email officialkeydir.in@gmail.com with subject [SECURITY] — see the Responsible Disclosure page.
Everything else
Email for sensitive or legal matters, the Contact page for general questions.

SECTION_03 Response Times

  • Email is typically answered within a week; security and data-removal requests are prioritized.
  • KeyDir is a small team. If something is urgent, mark the subject line clearly.

SECTION_04 Report Abuse

Fraudulent vendors, false listings, harassment, vote manipulation, and anyone selling organic placement on the platform should be reported. A good report includes the listing URL, what happened, and any evidence.

Full guidance — what counts as abuse, what not to report here, and what happens after you report — is on the dedicated Report Abuse page.

Send reports to officialkeydir.in@gmail.com with subject [ABUSE].

SECTION_05 Request Data Removal

You can request removal of data about you or your business from the platform — listings you did not request, outdated personal details, or your own contributions — subject to the Privacy Policy.

The full procedure, including what to include, what happens next, and the limits of removal, is on the dedicated Data Removal page.

To make a request, email officialkeydir.in@gmail.com with subject [DATA], identifying the listing or page and your relationship to it.

SECTION_06 What We Can't Help With

  • Order problems — the KeyDir Application does not sell, ship, or process payments. Contact the vendor directly, and consult the applicable consumer-protection channel in your jurisdiction if needed.
  • Refunds — only the seller can refund you.
  • Legal disputes — the platform is not a mediator or a party to your transactions. See the Disclaimer.

If we can help point you the right way, we will.
