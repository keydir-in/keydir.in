SECTION_03 Response Times

  • GitHub issues are typically triaged within a few days.
  • Email is typically answered within a week; security and data-removal requests are prioritized.
  • This is a volunteer project. If something is urgent, mark the subject line clearly.

SECTION_04 Report Abuse

Use this channel to report:

  • A vendor, builder, or brand acting fraudulently or failing to deliver.
  • A listing that is false, misleading, or impersonating a real business.
  • Harassment, doxxing, or abuse by any user of the service.
  • Vote or review manipulation.
  • Anyone claiming to sell listing placement on KEYDIR.
  • Copyright infringement (see the DMCA page).

When you report, include:

  1. The listing, page, or account involved.
  2. What happened, when, and the impact.
  3. Any evidence — order numbers, screenshots, links (redact personal data).
  4. Your contact details so we can follow up.

Send reports to officialkeydir.in@gmail.com with subject [ABUSE], or use the GitHub issue tracker where it is safe to discuss publicly. Reports are reviewed by the maintainers. Retaliation against reporters is itself a violation of the Community Guidelines.

SECTION_05 Request Data Removal

You can request removal of data about you or your business from the directory, subject to the Privacy Policy. This covers listings you did not request, outdated personal details, or deletion of your own data.

To make a request:

  1. Email officialkeydir.in@gmail.com with subject [DATA].
  2. Identify the data — the listing URL or page, and what you want removed.
  3. State your relationship to the data (e.g. "I am the owner of this listing" or "this contains my personal information").
  4. Provide reasonable proof of identity or authority where the request is not obviously about yourself.

What to expect:

  • We confirm receipt within a few days and act on valid requests promptly, usually within 30 days.
  • Public project records (such as GitHub history) may retain references for archival purposes; we will remove them from the live service and explain any limits.
  • Where a request conflicts with our legal obligations, we will explain why.
  • If we decline a request, you may appeal by replying to our response.
⚖
Where data-protection law applies to you (such as India's DPDP Act, 2023, or the EU GDPR), the rights described in the Privacy Policy also apply. This procedure is our practical mechanism for exercising those rights; legal review is recommended before relying on it in a formal matter.

SECTION_06 What We Can't Help With

  • Order problems — KEYDIR does not sell, ship, or process payments. Contact the vendor directly, and consult the applicable consumer-protection channel in your jurisdiction if needed.
  • Refunds — only the seller can refund you.
  • Legal disputes — KEYDIR is not a mediator or a party to your transactions. See the Disclaimer.

If we can help point you the right way, we will.
